S'mores

Back to Recipes

Description

S'mores are a classic treat to have on camping trips, a cold night by the fire, or just a simple snack to enjoy.

Ingredients

  • Graham crackers
  • Marshmallows
  • Hershey's Chocolate bars or your other favorite chocolate for a twist

Instructions

  1. Split graham crackers into 2 equal halves.
  2. Place half of the Hershey's milk chocolate bar on one of the graham cracker halves.
  3. Roast your marshmallow with a bamboo stick over a heat source until it is golden brown.
  4. Place the marshmallow on the chocolate and then cover with the other graham cracker half.
  5. Push down on the cracker just enough to secure the marshmallow and then slide the bamboo stick out of the marshmallow.
