Cowboy Nachos

Back to Recipes

Description

Cowboy nachos are a delicious and satisfying snack that's perfect for game day or any time you're in the mood for something spicy and cheesy.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup tortilla chips
  • 1/2 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped jalapeños
  • 1/4 cup sour cream

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until no longer pink. Drain excess fat.
  2. Add the taco seasoning and water according to package instructions. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  4. In a baking dish, layer the tortilla chips, beef mixture, and shredded cheese.
  5. Bake for 10-15 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Top with diced tomatoes, onion, and jalapeños. Serve with sour cream.
