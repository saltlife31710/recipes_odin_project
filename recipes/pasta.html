Cheesy Pasta

Back to Recipes

Description

Cheesy pasta is a classic comfort food that is easy to make and loved by everyone. This recipe is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or a cozy meal on a cold night.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta of your choice
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the flour and whisk until smooth, about 1-2 minutes.
  3. Gradually add the milk, whisking constantly until the mixture thickens, about 5 minutes.
  4. Remove the saucepan from heat and stir in the shredded cheddar cheese until melted and smooth. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Add the cooked pasta to the cheese sauce and toss until well coated. Serve immediately.
